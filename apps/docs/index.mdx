Corridor is the payment ledger and router that **licensed payment companies** run in their own environment, on their own keys. It runs cross-border stablecoin payouts across the providers a company already uses, **exactly once**, and keeps **one set of books that matches the chain**, from the customer's funding to the beneficiary's bank credit.

Corridor doesn't hold money, provide liquidity or carry licences. Payout partners such as Paj do that. Corridor is the system that makes a payout across several of them reliable, traceable and auditable.

<Note>
Corridor is software, not a custodian or a money transmitter. It runs in the customer's environment and signs through the customer's own KMS or MPC ([Keys and signers](/architecture/keys-and-signers)). Customers keep their licences, their KYC, their funds and their end-customer relationships, and they start [read-only](/flows/read-only-pilot): no keys, nothing moves.
</Note>

## Who it's for

Licensed remittance operators, payout companies, PSPs and business-FX firms that send stablecoin payouts through **two or more providers or chains**. That is where payouts stall halfway, retries pay twice and fees go unexplained. A company with one provider on one chain is well served by that provider's dashboard; Corridor is for the companies that have outgrown it.

## How it works

<Frame caption="Every flow is a collection into the Tempo hub or a payout out of it.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-light.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-dark.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
</Frame>

Every flow is either a **collection** into the Tempo hub or a **payout** out of it. A diaspora transfer into Lagos is a euro collection followed by a naira payout; a Nigerian importer paying a supplier in Shenzhen is a naira collection followed by a yuan payout. Adding a region means adding one payout partner, and every other region can reach it: **N integrations instead of N×N corridors**.

Each payout is a durable saga. Every external step (a bridge deposit, a partner order, a funding transfer) is recorded before Corridor waits on it, every movement is booked from the transaction that caused it, and the same 32-byte reference rides every leg.
