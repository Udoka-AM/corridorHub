Corridor is settlement and treasury infrastructure for **licensed payment companies** (PSPs, remittance operators, payout companies and the banks behind importers) moving money into and out of Africa. Instead of pre-funding a bank account in every country, a customer holds **one stablecoin balance on Tempo**, and Corridor routes each payment to the local rail that pays it out, reconciles every movement across every chain, and pays suppliers privately on Zcash when the amounts should not be public.

<Note>
Corridor is infrastructure, not a money transmitter. Customers keep their licences, their KYC and their end-customer relationships. Corridor holds, moves and reconciles their treasury.
</Note>

## How the pieces fit

<Frame caption="Fig. 02 · Every flow is a collection into the Tempo hub or a payout out of it.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-light.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-dark.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
</Frame>

Every flow is either a **collection** into the Tempo hub or a **payout** out of it. A Nigerian importer paying a supplier in Shenzhen is a naira collection followed by a yuan payout; a diaspora transfer into Lagos is a euro collection followed by a naira payout. Adding a region means adding one partner, and it can immediately reach every other region: **N integrations instead of N×N corridors**.
