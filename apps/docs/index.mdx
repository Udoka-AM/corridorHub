Corridor is the payment ledger and router that **licensed payment companies** (PSPs, remittance operators, payout companies and the banks behind importers) run themselves for cross-border payments, starting with the trade corridors into and out of Africa. It moves stablecoin payouts over **Tempo** and the partners that pay out locally, and matches **every payout from customer funding to beneficiary bank credit**, whichever provider and chain carried it. Suppliers can be paid privately on Zcash, with a viewing key for the auditor.

<Note>
Corridor is software, not a custodian or a money transmitter. It runs in the customer's environment and signs with the customer's own keys ([Keys and signers](/architecture/keys-and-signers)). Customers keep their licences, their KYC, their funds and their end-customer relationships. They start [read-only](/flows/read-only-pilot): no keys, nothing moves.
</Note>

## How the pieces fit

<Frame caption="Fig. 02 · Every flow is a collection into the Tempo hub or a payout out of it.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-light.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/hub-and-spoke-dark.svg" alt="Hub and spoke network with the Tempo hub at the centre" />
</Frame>

Every flow is either a **collection** into the Tempo hub or a **payout** out of it. A Nigerian importer paying a supplier in Shenzhen is a naira collection followed by a yuan payout; a diaspora transfer into Lagos is a euro collection followed by a naira payout. Adding a region means adding one partner, and it can immediately reach every other region: **N integrations instead of N×N corridors**.
