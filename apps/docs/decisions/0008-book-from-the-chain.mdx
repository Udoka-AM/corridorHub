## Context

Memo-matched reconciliation proves that every Corridor-referenced movement was booked. It does not prove completeness: fees, sponsored reserves and owner top-ups move vault balances without carrying a reference, and a ledger that ignores them drifts from the chain while still balancing perfectly.

## Decision

**Book every vault movement from the chain evidence that proves it.** Every ledger entry for an on-chain movement is keyed by its transaction, and fees, reserves and rounding are booked from what the network reports rather than from what the code intended.

- Stellar charges fees in XLM. With `unreferenced` set, `StellarObserver` reports the XLM fee each outbound transaction charged, account funding and sponsored reserves, and each is booked to `expense:gas` or `equity:owner` keyed by that transaction.
- Anchor rounding is booked as dust held for the customer, not written off.
- Unidentified deposits stay `unreferenced` and are never credited automatically: a payment sent without a memo may be a customer's money.
- A vault check compares each vault's on-chain balance with the ledger, and `checkInvariants()` re-verifies globally that every entry balances per asset and that the balance projection equals a full recompute from postings.

## Consequences

- A vault's on-chain balance equals the ledger to the unit, fees included. That is only possible because the ledger keeps the chain's own precision ([ADR 0012](/decisions/0012-seven-decimal-ledger)).
- Booking is idempotent: each movement is keyed by its transaction, under a unique `(venue, external_ref)`.
- Movements are processed in chain order, so a top-up is booked before the fees it paid for.
- Observers must resume from a stored paging token, or a restart double-books or skips.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Estimate fees at send time:** misses retries, compensations and any transaction the code did not anticipate.
- **Periodic balance adjustments:** hide the cause of drift instead of booking it, and an auditor cannot tell an adjustment from an error.
