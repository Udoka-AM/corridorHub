## Context

The prototype kept 6 decimal places, because its hub and its spokes were all 6 dp chains. Stellar carries every balance to **7** decimal places as a decimal string. A 6 dp ledger in front of a 7 dp chain cannot represent a stroop, so either the ledger rounds — and a vault can never equal the books exactly — or the rounding error is hidden in a tolerance.

Reconciliation is the product. A tolerance in the comparison is a hole in it.

## Decision

**The ledger keeps 7 decimal places, the hub's own precision.** `DIGITAL_ASSETS` gives USDC, USDT, PYUSD, MGUSD, EURC and XLM 7 dp, amounts are integer minor units of that, and converting a Stellar decimal string into the ledger is formatting rather than rounding.

**Spokes with fewer decimals convert through `rescale`, which refuses any conversion that would drop value:**

```ts
rescale(amount, from, to)  // 7 dp → 6 dp throws if the seventh decimal is not zero
```

USDC is 6 dp on Solana and Base, and scales into 7 dp exactly. Going the other way, an amount carrying a seventh decimal surfaces as an error instead of disappearing.

## Consequences

- A vault's on-chain balance can equal the ledger to the stroop, which is what makes the vault check meaningful ([ADR 0008](/decisions/0008-book-from-the-chain)).
- There are no floats anywhere in monetary code, and `numeric(78,0)` postings are wide enough for any on-chain amount.
- A payout whose amount cannot be paid on a 6 dp spoke fails loudly at the edge rather than silently losing a stroop of a customer's money. In practice that is a stroop of dust, which is exactly the size of error that is easiest to ignore and hardest to find later.
- Fiat keeps its own decimals (`FIAT[...].decimals`): 2 for NGN and KES, 0 for XOF and UGX.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep 6 dp and round at the Stellar edge:** the books can then never equal the chain, and every reconciliation needs a tolerance.
- **Decimal strings in the ledger:** invites comparison and arithmetic bugs that integers make impossible.
- **Round silently on the way to a 6 dp spoke:** the amounts are small, which is why nobody would notice the day it was a bug rather than dust.
