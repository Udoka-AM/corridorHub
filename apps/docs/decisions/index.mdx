Each record states the decision and the reasoning behind it, so a reviewer can disagree with the reasoning rather than guess at it. These are the decisions the code embodies today; several are cited by number from the source.

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [0001](/decisions/0001-usdc-on-stellar-settlement-unit) | USDC on Stellar is the hub's unit of account | Accepted |
| [0002](/decisions/0002-hub-enforced-by-planner) | The Stellar hub is enforced by the route planner | Accepted |
| [0003](/decisions/0003-reference-in-memo-hash) | The 32-byte reference rides `MEMO_HASH`; reconciliation is a join, not an index | Accepted |
| [0004](/decisions/0004-saga-over-two-phase-commit) | A compensating saga rather than two-phase commit | Accepted |
| [0005](/decisions/0005-confidential-lane-deferred) | The confidential lane waits for Stellar confidential tokens | Accepted |
| [0006](/decisions/0006-anchors-as-sep-spokes) | Anchors are the fiat spokes, reached over the SEPs | Accepted |
| [0007](/decisions/0007-issuer-is-part-of-asset-identity) | The issuer is part of the asset's identity; unverified issuers stay unset | Accepted |
| [0008](/decisions/0008-book-from-the-chain) | Fees, reserves and funding are booked from what the chain shows | Accepted |
| [0009](/decisions/0009-customer-held-keys) | Customers hold the keys; Corridor signs through their KMS or MPC | Accepted |
| [0010](/decisions/0010-issuer-neutral-stablecoins) | USDC, USDT, PYUSD, MGUSD and EURC are each first-class | Accepted |
| [0011](/decisions/0011-read-only-first) | A customer starts read-only; moving money is the second step | Accepted |
| [0012](/decisions/0012-seven-decimal-ledger) | The ledger keeps Stellar's seven decimal places | Accepted |
| [0013](/decisions/0013-ramp-and-hub-share-one-core) | Ramp and the Hub are two front doors on one core | Accepted |

<Note>
Corridor's earlier prototype settled on Tempo and reached its payout rails through bespoke provider APIs. Numbers are kept where the decision survived the move to Stellar (0002, 0004, 0008, 0009, 0010, 0011); 0001, 0003 and 0005 are the same question answered differently on Stellar, and 0006, 0007 and 0012 are decisions the hub itself forced.
</Note>
