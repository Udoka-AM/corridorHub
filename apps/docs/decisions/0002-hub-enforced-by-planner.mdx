## Context

A network of bilateral corridors grows as N×N: every new region needs a link to every other. Treasury, reconciliation and visibility fragment the same way.

Stellar makes the hub shape unusually cheap to hold to, because **anchors settle on Stellar itself**. The fiat spoke touches the hub directly, so insisting on the hub costs no extra hop on the common path.

## Decision

**Stellar is the hub, enforced in code.** `planRoute` throws `NoRouteError` for a payout not funded from the hub or a collection that does not land on it:

```ts
// packages/routing/src/index.ts
if (purpose === "payout" && !onHub(req.source))
  throw new NoRouteError("payouts are funded from the customer's balance on the Stellar hub");
if (purpose === "collection" && !onHub(req.destination))
  throw new NoRouteError("collections settle into the Stellar hub");
```

A corridor is just a currency pair and the reference encodes the pair ([ADR 0003](/decisions/0003-reference-in-memo-hash)), so a new corridor such as `GHS-HKD` needs no registry change.

## Consequences

- **N integrations, not N×N corridors.** Because anchors speak the same SEPs ([ADR 0006](/decisions/0006-anchors-as-sep-spokes)), adding a region is a domain and an asset rather than an integration, and every existing spoke can reach it.
- Every dollar is settled, held and reconciled in one ledger, visible to treasury in one place.
- Hub enforcement is a correctness property, not a convention: it is tested in `packages/routing/src/routing.test.ts`.
- A currency whose only licensed partner settles off Stellar costs one extra hop. Naira is the case that matters, and it sits in `OFF_STELLAR_PAYOUT_CURRENCIES` until a licensed naira anchor is live on Stellar; moving it onto the hub is then one line.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Direct spoke-to-spoke routes when cheaper:** saves a hop, loses the single treasury and the single reconciliation point, which are the product.
- **Hub as a convention, enforced in review:** the first exception under delivery pressure is the one that costs a day of reconciliation.
