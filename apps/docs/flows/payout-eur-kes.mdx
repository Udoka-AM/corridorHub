A European payout company pays a beneficiary in Nairobi. Its customer balance already sits on the Stellar hub as USDC.

This is the shape `pnpm stellar:payout` runs on testnet: the route, the saga, the Stellar payment and every ledger entry are the real ones; the anchor is `MockPartner`, because **no anchor is signed**, so no Kenyan shilling actually moves.

## The plan

```text
ledger.reserve → partner.payout
```

That is the whole route, on testnet and on mainnet. The anchor settles on the hub, so nothing stands between the customer's balance and the beneficiary's currency — no bridge, no second chain, no intermediate vault.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reserve">
    The amount moves from the customer's available balance into a hold, so it cannot be spent twice while the payout is in flight.

    | Account | Debit | Credit |
    |---|---|---|
    | `liability:customer:eu-payco:EUR-KES:USDC:stellar` | 7 USDC | |
    | `liability:customer_hold:eu-payco:EUR-KES:USDC:stellar` | | 7 USDC |

    Both accounts are `no_overdraft`, so a payout larger than the balance is refused inside the same database transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate and quote">
    `SepAnchor` fetches the anchor's `stellar.toml`, gets a SEP-10 challenge, **verifies it** (built by the anchor's own `SIGNING_KEY`, naming this vault, on the expected network) and only then signs it. The JWT is cached.

    SEP-38 then gives a firm quote: sell `stellar:USDC:<issuer>`, buy `iso4217:KES`, `context: "sep31"`. The beneficiary amount is known before anything moves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    `POST /transactions` with the quote, the beneficiary fields and the Corridor Reference in `fields.transaction.reference`. The anchor returns `stellar_account_id` and **its own** `stellar_memo`. The order link is recorded before anything is sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund it, exactly once">
    A Stellar payment from the treasury vault to the anchor's receiving account, carrying **the anchor's memo** — on this leg Corridor's reference cannot ride in `MEMO_HASH`, so it travels in the SEP-31 fields and the join key is `(anchor, transaction id)`.

    Before sending, the leg asks Horizon whether an identical movement already landed, so a retry after a crash finds the payment instead of sending it again. The funding transaction is then recorded durably.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait">
    SEP-31 has no webhook, so the saga polls `GET /transactions/{id}` until the anchor reports `completed`. The anchor pays KES to the beneficiary's account or mobile wallet on its own rail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book it">
    | Account | Debit | Credit |
    |---|---|---|
    | `liability:customer_hold:eu-payco:EUR-KES:USDC:stellar` | 7 USDC | |
    | `asset:vault:treasury:USDC:stellar` | | 7 USDC |

    Keyed on `partner_fiat` / `${anchor}:${orderId}`, so it can only be booked once. Anchor rounding is booked as dust held for the customer rather than written off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile">
    The Stellar payment is an `Observation` from Horizon; the completed order is an `Observation` from the anchor. Both match the ledger entries keyed by them, and `checkInvariants()` confirms every entry balances per asset.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Net effect

| Account | Change |
|---|---|
| Customer available balance | −7 USDC |
| Customer hold | 0 |
| Treasury vault on Stellar | −7 USDC |
| `expense:gas` | + the XLM fee the transaction paid, booked from the transaction |
| Beneficiary | + KES at the quoted rate |

## If it fails

- **Before funding:** the saga compensates in reverse order. The hold is released and the customer ends exactly where they started. See [Failure and compensation](/flows/failure-and-compensation).
- **After funding:** the saga stops at `manual_review`. The money is at the anchor until its refund is confirmed, and unwinding Corridor's books before that would describe a sequence that never happened.

## Funded in something other than USDC

A balance in EURC or MGUSD plans as `reserve → stellar.pathPayment → partner.payout`, converting on Stellar's own order books. The planner produces that route; the path-payment leg is [not written yet](/integrations/stellar#on-ledger-fx).
