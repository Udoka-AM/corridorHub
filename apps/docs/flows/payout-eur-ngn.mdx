A European payout company pays Ada Obi in Lagos. Its customer balance already sits on the Tempo hub as pathUSD. This is the run recorded on testnet on 29 September 2026: **2 pathUSD → ₦3,060 at 1,530**.

## The plan

| Environment | Route |
|---|---|
| Testnet (recorded) | `ledger.reserve → bridge.simulated (Tempo → Solana) → partner.payout (Solana → NGN)` |
| Mainnet | `ledger.reserve → across.bridge (Tempo → Base) → across.bridge (Base → Solana) → partner.payout (Solana → NGN)` |

On mainnet the payout takes two Across hops, because Paj settles on Solana and Across has no direct Tempo → Solana route.

A payout can be funded from a pathUSD or a USDC.e balance on Tempo. Across takes either one directly, so neither needs a swap first. Each is booked in its own asset, and gas is paid in pathUSD from the owner's float. Every hop books the amount that actually arrived and Across's fee. The partner is funded with exactly what its order asks for, and any rounding dust is held for the customer. Other stablecoins (USDT, EURC, PYUSD) are watched and reconciled today; paying out from them needs a swap leg first, and that is on the [roadmap](/status/roadmap).

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reserve">
    The amount moves from the customer's available balance into a hold, so it cannot be spent twice while the payout is in flight.

    | Account | Debit | Credit |
    |---|---|---|
    | `customer:eu-payco-demo:EUR-NGN:pathUSD:tempo` (available) | 2 | |
    | `customer_hold:eu-payco-demo:EUR-NGN:pathUSD:tempo` | | 2 |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle out of the hub">
    pathUSD leaves the treasury vault with the Corridor Reference in the TIP-20 memo ([0x43e7f0ba…](https://explore.testnet.tempo.xyz/tx/0x43e7f0bac5488ac23adc9120c988f310f27b4f5ca17dca5bdc0497f8c2109e52)). Two entries book the two sides of the bridge through the `usd-peg` clearing pair: the Tempo side keyed by the log, the Solana side by the fill.

    | Account | Debit | Credit |
    |---|---|---|
    | `clearing:usd-peg:pathUSD:tempo` | 2 pathUSD | |
    | `vault:treasury:pathUSD:tempo` | | 2 pathUSD |
    | `vault:solana-egress:USDC:solana` | 2 USDC | |
    | `clearing:usd-peg:USDC:solana` | | 2 USDC |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the partner order">
    `openPayout` returns a one-off funding address. The order link is recorded before anything is sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the partner, exactly once">
    USDC goes from the Solana egress vault to the funding address with `corridor:<ref>` as an SPL Memo ([nDGHfRgM…](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/nDGHfRgMgK1Xc8gjE3bqUvgfp5qP13HnP6nTtLQGBSqaLLtdDfg2nXYPEzbHfcwpYjv1xQFedMcRh3KkJyRZicH?cluster=devnet)). The funding transaction is recorded, so a retry will not fund twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay out">
    The partner pays ₦3,060 to the beneficiary and confirms with a signed webhook. The payout is booked against the partner order:

    | Account | Debit | Credit |
    |---|---|---|
    | `customer_hold:eu-payco-demo:EUR-NGN:pathUSD:tempo` | 2 pathUSD | |
    | `clearing:usd-peg:pathUSD:tempo` | | 2 pathUSD |
    | `clearing:usd-peg:USDC:solana` | 2 USDC | |
    | `vault:solana-egress:USDC:solana` | | 2 USDC |

    Both sides of the `usd-peg` pair are now back to zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile">
    The Tempo log, the Solana memo transfer and the partner webhook each become an `Observation` and match the ledger entries keyed by them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Net effect

| Account | Change |
|---|---|
| Customer available balance | −2 pathUSD |
| Treasury vault on Tempo | −2 pathUSD (moved out of the hub) |
| Clearing pairs | 0 |
| Beneficiary | +₦3,060 |
