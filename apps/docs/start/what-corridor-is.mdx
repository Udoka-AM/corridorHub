## The product in one paragraph

Corridor is the payment ledger and router a licensed payment company **runs itself, on its own keys**. It doesn't hold funds, provide liquidity or carry licences; it runs payouts across the anchors and providers that do, exactly once, and keeps books that match the chain.

A single EUR → KES payout crosses a bank collection, a stablecoin issuer, an anchor and the beneficiary's bank, each with its own identifier. Corridor follows it through all of it with a 32-byte **Corridor Reference** carried in Stellar's `MEMO_HASH`, matches it from funding to bank credit, and puts everything that did not match in an exceptions queue.

When Corridor runs a payout, it settles on **Stellar** and pays out through the anchor that serves the destination. Customers start **read-only**: Corridor watches the Stellar accounts they already run and matches their own payout records, with no keys and nothing moving.

## What it replaces

| Today | With Corridor |
|---|---|
| Fiat pre-funded in a bank account in every country | One USDC balance on Stellar, topped up when the forecast says so |
| T+1 to T+3 through correspondent banks | Settlement on the hub in seconds; minutes end to end through the anchor's local rail |
| A new banking setup per corridor (N×N) | One anchor per region; every spoke reaches every spoke (N) |
| Spreadsheets and statement matching across providers | Every payout matched from funding to bank credit, with an exceptions queue |
| Retries that pay twice, payouts that stall halfway | Every external step recorded before it is awaited: a retry resumes, a gap stops for a person |
| Stuck or short-paid payouts found when a customer complains | `missing` and `short` flagged the same day |
| Locked into one provider's dashboard | Anchors become interchangeable spokes; the ledger and the history stay |

## What Corridor is not

- **Not a custodian or a money transmitter.** Corridor runs in the customer's environment and signs with the customer's own KMS or MPC keys; it never holds customer funds. The licensed customer holds the FX permissions, the KYC and the end-customer relationship. For outbound flows from regulated markets (for example Form M for Nigerian imports), Corridor carries the compliance references in the saga data and produces the reconciliation trail as audit evidence.
- **Not a wallet or an exchange.** Customers do not trade on Corridor; it settles and reconciles their payments.
- **Not an anchor.** Anchors hold the licences, the local rails and the float in each market. Corridor is the ledger and router in front of them.
- **Not tied to one stablecoin.** USDC is the hub's unit of account, and USDT, PYUSD, MGUSD and EURC are each first-class assets ([ADR 0010](/decisions/0010-issuer-neutral-stablecoins)).
- **Not endorsed by anyone.** Corridor is an independent build on Stellar.

## Platform status

Every row links to what backs it. Nothing here claims mainnet.

| Capability | State | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Reference carried in `MEMO_HASH`, read back off Horizon | **Run on Stellar testnet** | A payment with the reference as `MEMO_HASH`, found again on the account's own payment stream and decoded whole — [evidence](/status/evidence#stellar-testnet) |
| A whole payout through the saga engine | **Run on Stellar testnet** | Route planned, balance reserved, anchor order opened and funded exactly once with a real Stellar payment, every movement booked, no invariant violations — [evidence](/status/evidence#stellar-testnet) |
| Hub-and-spoke routing over 32 currencies | **Built and tested** | The planner enforces the hub and rejects anything else; every route shape is a test |
| Double-entry ledger at 7 dp | **Built and tested** | Entries balance per asset, no-overdraft accounts cannot go negative, each external movement books once |
| Saga engine with compensation and fencing | **Built and tested** | Reverse-order compensation, `manual_review` after money leaves, and a race test that proves a lapsed lease writes nothing |
| SEP anchor adapter (SEP-1/10/12/38/31) | **Built, against a stubbed anchor** | The SEP-10 challenge is verified before the vault signs it; the JWT is cached; SEP-31 is polled because it has no webhook |
| Customer-held signing | **Built and tested** | One signer interface; AWS KMS and Turnkey adapters; Stellar and Solana share one Ed25519 key |
| Read-only reconciliation | **Built** | Watches Stellar accounts by id and matches the customer's own payout records; exceptions queue and CSV |
| Issuer-neutral stablecoins | **Built** | USDC, USDT, PYUSD, MGUSD and EURC each booked in their own asset, matched on code *and* issuer |
| **A signed anchor** | **Not done** | No real local currency has moved on Stellar |
| **SEP-6 collections** | **Not built** | `openCollection` raises rather than guessing at the flow |
| **The `cctp.transfer` leg** | **Not built** | Declared in the route graph; testnet uses `bridge.simulated`, whose Stellar side is real |
| **MGUSD, PYUSD, USDT issuers** | **Unset on purpose** | Each stays unset until verified against the issuer's own `stellar.toml` |
| **Treasury console** | **Not built for Stellar** | The prototype's console read Tempo and Solana and was removed; `pnpm watch:report` covers the read-only reporting |
| **A mainnet run** | **Not done** | Code paths are identical; nothing has run on Stellar mainnet |
