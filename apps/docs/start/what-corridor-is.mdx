## The product in one paragraph

Corridor gives a licensed payment company a **single stablecoin treasury on Tempo** that funds every corridor it serves. Money comes in through local ramp partners (collections), sits as pathUSD on the Tempo hub, and goes out to the rail that pays the beneficiary: a Nigerian bank account, a Hong Kong supplier account, Pix in Brazil, or a shielded Zcash address (payouts). Each movement carries a 32-byte **Corridor Reference** in the native memo field of every chain it touches, so the ledger, the chains and the partners reconcile automatically.

## What it replaces

| Today | With Corridor |
|---|---|
| Fiat pre-funded in a bank account in every country | One pathUSD balance on Tempo, topped up when the forecast says so |
| T+1 to T+3 through correspondent banks | 0.6 s finality on the hub; minutes end to end through partners |
| A new banking setup per corridor (N×N) | One partner per region; every spoke reaches every spoke (N) |
| Spreadsheets and statement matching | Automatic matching on a reference carried in every chain's memo |
| Supplier prices visible on public chains | Shielded batches on Zcash, with a viewing key scoped to one customer and period |

## What Corridor is not

- **Not a money transmitter.** The licensed customer holds the FX permissions, the KYC and the end-customer relationship. For outbound flows from regulated markets (for example Form M for Nigerian imports), Corridor carries the compliance references in the saga data and produces the reconciliation trail as audit evidence.
- **Not a wallet or an exchange.** Customers do not trade on Corridor; it settles and reconciles their payments.
- **Not a new chain.** Corridor composes existing chains, each for the job it does best: Tempo for settlement, Solana and Base for partner liquidity, Zcash for privacy.
