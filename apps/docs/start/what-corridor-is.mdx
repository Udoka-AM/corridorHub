## The product in one paragraph

Corridor is the payment ledger and router a licensed payment company **runs itself, with its own keys**. A single EUR → NGN payout today crosses a bank collection, one or more stablecoin providers, several chains, a bridge, a local payout partner and the beneficiary's bank, each with its own ids and dashboard. Corridor follows each payout through all of it with a 32-byte **Corridor Reference** carried in every chain's native memo, matches it from funding to bank credit, and puts everything that did not match in an exceptions queue.

When Corridor moves the money, it settles on **Tempo** and pays out through the partner that serves the destination: a Nigerian bank account, a Hong Kong supplier account, Pix in Brazil, or a shielded Zcash address. Customers start **read-only**: Corridor watches the wallets they already run and matches their own payout records, with no keys and nothing moving.

## What it replaces

| Today | With Corridor |
|---|---|
| Fiat pre-funded in a bank account in every country | One pathUSD balance on Tempo, topped up when the forecast says so |
| T+1 to T+3 through correspondent banks | 0.6 s finality on the hub; minutes end to end through partners |
| A new banking setup per corridor (N×N) | One partner per region; every spoke reaches every spoke (N) |
| Spreadsheets and statement matching across providers | Every payout matched from funding to bank credit, with an exceptions queue |
| Stuck or short-paid payouts found when a customer complains | `missing` and `short` flagged the same day |
| Locked into one provider's dashboard | Providers become interchangeable venues; the ledger and history stay |
| Supplier prices visible on public chains | Shielded batches on Zcash, with a viewing key scoped to one customer and period |

## What Corridor is not

- **Not a custodian or a money transmitter.** Corridor runs in the customer's environment and signs with the customer's own KMS or MPC keys; it never holds customer funds. The licensed customer holds the FX permissions, the KYC and the end-customer relationship. For outbound flows from regulated markets (for example Form M for Nigerian imports), Corridor carries the compliance references in the saga data and produces the reconciliation trail as audit evidence.
- **Not a wallet or an exchange.** Customers do not trade on Corridor; it settles and reconciles their payments.
- **Not a new chain or a new rail.** Corridor composes existing chains and providers, each for the job it does best: Tempo for settlement, Solana and Base for partner liquidity, Zcash for privacy, and payout and liquidity providers (Paj, Yellow Card, Conduit, Mansa and others) as venues.
- **Not tied to one stablecoin.** USDC, USDT, PYUSD and EURC are first-class alongside pathUSD ([ADR 0010](/decisions/0010-issuer-neutral-stablecoins)).
