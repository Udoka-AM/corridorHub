Pick the route that matches what you need to decide.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Stellar reviewers">
    You have ten minutes and you want to know whether the Stellar work is real.

    <Card title="Ten-minute review" icon="timer" href="/start/ten-minute-review">
      Six things to read in order, two commands to run, the three files worth opening, and the whole list of what is deliberately not built.
    </Card>

    The short version: `MEMO_HASH` is exactly 32 bytes and anchors settle on Stellar itself, and those two facts shape the entire design. The hub is enforced in the planner, the ledger keeps Stellar's 7 decimal places, and reconciliation is a join on a watched account's own payment stream rather than an index.
