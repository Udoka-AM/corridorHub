[ 02 / The problem ]

Cross-border payments in Africa run on trapped capital.

Payment companies pre-fund bank accounts in every country, wait days on correspondent banks, and reconcile statements by hand. It happens in both directions: money sent home, and money sent abroad to pay for imports.

~9%

Average cost of sending $200 to Sub-Saharan Africa, the most expensive region in the world. The global average is 6.49%.

$179B

Chinese exports to Africa in 2024. Every one is a payment an African business has to get abroad.

T+1–3

Typical correspondent settlement. Balances sit idle in foreign accounts while treasury teams wait.

Sources: World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide, Q1 2025 · China General Administration of Customs, 2024.

One treasury. Every corridor. Books that close themselves.

Corridor is infrastructure for licensed players. Our customers keep their licences and their end-customer relationships. Corridor holds, moves and reconciles their money.

[ 04 / How a payment moves ]

From quote to closed books in five steps.

Every payout runs as a saga. Each step has a matching reversal, so a failure partway through unwinds cleanly on-chain instead of leaving money stranded.

  1. 01

    Quote

    The PSP asks for EUR→NGN. Corridor returns a firm rate with a time limit and reserves the customer's funds.

    ledger · reserve
  2. 02

    Settle

    Value moves on Tempo with the payout's reference in the transfer memo. Final in under a second.

    Tempo · transferWithMemo
  3. 03

    Route

    USDC bridges to the chain where the local partner settles. For naira that is Base, one hop.

    Across · Tempo → Base
  4. 04

    Pay out

    The partner pays the bank account and confirms with a signed webhook. Funding happens exactly once, even across retries.

    partner · signed webhook
  5. 05

    Reconcile

    Chain, partner and ledger records are matched by reference. Exceptions surface in the treasury console.

    recon · 3-way match

If a partner fails after it has been paid, the payout stops for human review rather than reversing automatically. The money is with the partner until its refund is confirmed, and the books say so.

No single person can move a vault. Each shielded vault's spend key exists only as three FROST shares, held by the customer's approver, its finance lead and Corridor. Any two sign a batch together; one alone is refused. It's verified on Zcash testnet with a 2-of-3 signed Ironwood batch.
